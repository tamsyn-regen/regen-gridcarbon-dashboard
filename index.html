Are electricity prices really cheaper when the carbon intensity of the grid is lowest?

Regen have developed this dashboard to track the correlation between grid carbon intensity and electricity prices over time. Select a time period, or a specific day. Octopus Agile prices are shown alongside GB wholesale market index prices (Elexon BMRS) for comparison. Please be patient while data loads.
